## Sector Overview

Nepal's banking sector remains the backbone of the country's financial system, representing over 60% of NEPSE's total market capitalization. With 20+ commercial banks, the sector has undergone significant consolidation in recent years.

## Key Metrics to Watch

### Capital Adequacy

Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB) has progressively raised minimum paid-up capital requirements, driving mergers and acquisitions across the sector. This consolidation trend is expected to strengthen balance sheets and improve operational efficiency.

### Credit Growth

Credit growth has moderated from the double-digit rates seen in previous years, reflecting NRB's tighter monetary policy stance. However, underlying demand for credit remains strong, driven by:

- Infrastructure development projects
- Growing SME sector
- Increasing consumer finance penetration

### Asset Quality

Non-performing loan (NPL) ratios have been a concern, particularly in the real estate and margin lending segments. Investors should closely monitor:

- Sector-wise credit concentration
- Provisioning coverage ratios
- Restructured loan portfolios

## Investment Thesis

Despite near-term headwinds, the long-term outlook for Nepal's banking sector remains positive. Key catalysts include:

1. **Digital transformation** reducing operational costs
2. **Financial inclusion** expanding the addressable market
3. **Merger benefits** creating stronger, more efficient institutions
4. **Economic growth** driving sustained credit demand

## Risks to Monitor

- Regulatory tightening beyond market expectations
- Deterioration in asset quality during economic slowdowns
- Interest rate volatility impacting net interest margins
- Political instability affecting business confidence

## Conclusion

Selective exposure to well-managed banks with strong capital positions, diversified loan books, and progressive digital strategies offers compelling value for patient investors.
