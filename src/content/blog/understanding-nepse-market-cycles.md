## Introduction

Nepal's stock market, represented by the Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE), has exhibited distinct cyclical patterns since its inception. Understanding these cycles is crucial for investors seeking to optimize their entry and exit points.

## Key Market Indicators

When analyzing NEPSE market cycles, several indicators stand out:

1. **Trading Volume Trends** - Sustained increases in trading volume often precede major price movements
2. **Foreign Investment Flows** - Changes in foreign portfolio investment patterns signal shifting sentiment
3. **Sectoral Rotation** - Capital movement between banking, hydropower, and insurance sectors reveals market maturity
4. **Monetary Policy Impact** - Nepal Rastra Bank's interest rate decisions directly influence market liquidity

## Historical Cycle Analysis

Over the past decade, NEPSE has experienced three major bull-bear cycles. Each cycle lasted approximately 3-4 years from trough to peak, with corrections typically retracing 40-60% of the preceding gains.

### Cycle Characteristics

- **Accumulation Phase**: Low volume, depressed valuations, negative sentiment
- **Markup Phase**: Increasing volume, improving fundamentals, growing optimism
- **Distribution Phase**: Peak volume, elevated valuations, euphoric sentiment
- **Decline Phase**: Falling volume initially, then panic selling, deteriorating sentiment

## Conclusion

By combining quantitative analysis of market data with qualitative assessment of macro conditions, investors can better position themselves within market cycles. The key is maintaining discipline and avoiding emotional decision-making during extreme market conditions.
